Ledgix

Accounting software for small businesses in Indonesia.

What Ledgix is

Ledgix keeps the books of a small company: sales and purchase invoices, cash and bank, journals, fixed assets and depreciation, Indonesian tax (PPN, PPh 23) and financial reports. Each customer runs Ledgix on their own server, so the company's accounting data stays with the company.

Offsite backup to Google Drive

An administrator can connect a Google account in Settings > Backup. Ledgix then sends its nightly backup there, so the books survive even if the server is lost.

  • Ledgix asks only for access to the files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or open anything else in the Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the customer's server before they are uploaded, with a passphrase only the customer holds.
  • The connection can be removed at any time in Ledgix, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
