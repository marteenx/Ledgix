Privacy Policy

Ledgix · Effective 23 October 2026 · Home

Ledgix is accounting software that each customer installs and runs on their own server. This policy explains what data Ledgix handles and, in particular, how it uses data from Google when a customer connects Google Drive for offsite backup.

1. Accounting data

The accounting data entered into Ledgix (customers, vendors, invoices, journals, attachments and so on) is stored in the database on the customer's own server. The Ledgix developer does not receive, collect or have access to this data.

2. Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional and is done by an administrator in Settings > Backup. When it is connected, Ledgix requests these permissions:

  • drive.file: to create, upload, list, download and delete only the files and folder that Ledgix itself creates (a folder named "Ledgix Backups"). Ledgix cannot see, open or change any other file in the Google Drive.
  • openid and email: to show which Google account is connected.

3. How the data is used

  • Ledgix uses Google Drive access only to store the customer's own backups: each night it uploads an encrypted copy of the database and of the attachments, deletes copies older than the retention period the customer chooses, and lets the customer download a backup to restore it.
  • Backups are encrypted on the customer's server, with a passphrase held by the customer, before they leave the server. Google and the Ledgix developer cannot read their contents.
  • The Google account email address is shown on the Backup page and used for nothing else.

4. Storage and security

The Google access token and the backup passphrase are stored only on the customer's server, encrypted. They are not sent to the Ledgix developer or to any other party. Communication with Google uses HTTPS.

5. Sharing

Ledgix does not sell, rent or share Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

Ledgix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Backups stay in the customer's Google Drive for the retention period set in Ledgix (30 days by default), and the newest seven are always kept. The customer can delete them in Google Drive at any time.
  • Choosing Disconnect in Ledgix revokes the Google access token and removes it from the server. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
